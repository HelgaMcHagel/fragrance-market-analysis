The segment labels are descriptive, relative to each gender category, and based on median thresholds. Community votes are used as an engagement proxy; they are not direct measures of sales, market share, or commercial performance.

METHODOLOGY

How to read the segments

Each category is segmented independently. An accord group is assigned using its mean rating and average votes per fragrance relative to that category’s medians.

Popular Performers

Community attention and mean rating are both at or above the category median.

High-Rated Niche Accords

Mean rating is at or above the category median, while community attention is below it.

Popular Underperformers

Community attention is at or above the category median, while mean rating is below it.

Low-Visibility, Lower-Rated Accords

Both community attention and mean rating are below the category medians.

Values equal to a median are assigned to the at-or-above group. “Niche” does not confirm a commercial market segment, and “underperformer” refers only to a below-median average rating.
